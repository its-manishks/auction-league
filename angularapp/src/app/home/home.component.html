How an auction runs

Three steps, split between two roles. Each person only sees the tools for their part.

  1. Set up the teams

    Create each franchise with a maximum budget. You can rename a team or change its budget at any time.

    Admin

  2. Build the player pool

    Add players with their age, category and bidding price. Every new player starts in the unsold pool.

    Admin

  3. Sell to teams

    Pick a player, choose a team and sell. Sales that would go over a team's budget are blocked, and players can be released back to the pool.

    Organizer
